Dr. Aslam Inamdar

BDS, MDS, International Mentor & Speaker

Dr. Aslam Inamdar is a leading expert in Full Mouth Rehabilitation, Dental Implants, Cosmetic Dentistry, and Digital Smile Design, with over 23 years of clinical excellence in advanced dental treatments.

Known for creating naturally aesthetic smiles using AI-powered smile design, in-house 3D planning, and advanced CAD/CAM technology, he has helped patients regain healthy, functional, and confident smiles with precision and comfort.

  • International mentor and speaker, having trained over 1,000 dental professionals in advanced digital dentistry techniques.
