What Is Root Canal Treatment?

Root canal treatment is a highly effective dental procedure designed to treat infected or damaged teeth while preserving the natural tooth structure. Using advanced techniques and modern equipment, the infected pulp is carefully removed, and the tooth is cleaned, disinfected, and sealed. This procedure not only eliminates pain but also prevents further infection, helping you maintain your natural smile.

Treatment Journey

Step-by-Step Process

1. Digital X-Ray & Diagnosis

High-resolution imaging to identify the exact location and extent of infection.

2. Painless Numbing

Computer-controlled anesthesia for a completely comfortable experience.

3. Pulp Removal & Cleaning

Infected tissue removed, canals shaped and disinfected under magnification.

4. Sealing & Crown Placement

Canal sealed with biocompatible material and protected with a ceramic crown.
