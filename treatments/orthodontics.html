Orthodontic Care

What Is Orthodontic Treatment?

Orthodontics focuses on correcting the alignment of teeth and jaws to improve both function and appearance. Whether through traditional braces or modern clear aligners, these treatments gradually move teeth into their ideal position. With advanced techniques and personalized treatment planning, orthodontics helps create a balanced, healthy, and aesthetically pleasing smile.

Options

Treatment Options

Metal Braces

Traditional fixed braces with modern low-profile brackets for effective alignment.

Ceramic Braces

Tooth-colored brackets that blend naturally for a discreet appearance.

Clear Aligners

Invisible, removable trays that straighten teeth without wires.

Self-Ligating Braces

Advanced braces requiring fewer adjustments and shorter treatment time.
