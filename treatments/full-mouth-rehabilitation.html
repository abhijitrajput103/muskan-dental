Total Restoration

What Is Full Mouth Rehabilitation?

A comprehensive restorative plan to rebuild both upper and lower dental arches. It restores collapsed bite height, corrects worn teeth, relieves jaw strain, and returns full chewing function.

Indications

Who Needs Full Reconstruction?

Multiple Missing or Broken Teeth

Restores ability to chew with strong, fixed porcelain/zirconia teeth.

Severe Teeth Grinding (Bruxism)

Rebuilds worn, flat teeth and restores youthful bite height.

TMJ & Chronic Jaw Pain

Relieves headaches and joint strain via balanced bite alignment.

Failing Older Dental Work

Upgrades ill-fitting bridges, broken crowns, or loose dentures.
