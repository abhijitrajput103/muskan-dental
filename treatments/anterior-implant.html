Implant Dentistry

What Is an Anterior Implant?

Anterior teeth (your front incisors and canines) are the focal point of your smile. An anterior dental implant involves placing a high-grade titanium post into the jawbone to replace the root of a missing front tooth, capped with a custom-matched porcelain crown. Designed to blend imperceptibly with surrounding natural teeth, this treatment preserves your facial contours and prevents jawbone degradation.
