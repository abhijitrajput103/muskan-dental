What Is Laser Gum Treatment?

Laser Gum Treatment is a modern, non-invasive alternative to traditional gum surgery designed to treat gum disease, gingivitis, and aesthetic gum unevenness. By utilizing highly focused light energy, the laser precisely targets and removes infected gum tissue while sterilizing the underlying area and killing deep-seated bacteria. Because it cauterizes tissue and blood vessels simultaneously, laser therapy offers maximum precision with virtually no pain, minimal bleeding, and significantly faster healing times than conventional surgical methods.

Treatment Journey

Laser Treatment Process

1. Diagnosis & Assessment

Comprehensive periodontal examination and gum pocket depth measurement.

2. Targeted Laser Application

Laser fiber energy directed into gum pockets to vaporize infected tissue.

3. Ultrasonic Cleaning

Removing hardened plaque and tartar from root surfaces beneath the gumline.

4. Tissue Sealing & Reattachment

Secondary laser pass to seal the pocket and encourage natural gum reattachment.
