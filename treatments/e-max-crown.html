All-Ceramic Technology

What Is an E-Max Crown?

An E-max (Lithium Disilicate) Crown is a state-of-the-art, all-ceramic restoration engineered for ultimate strength and natural translucency. Unlike traditional metal-based crowns, E-max crowns blend seamlessly with your natural enamel, reflecting light in the exact same way. They are an ideal solution for restoring fractured, severely decayed, or heavily stained teeth—particularly in visible areas of your smile—without compromising on structural durability.
