What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are one of the most advanced and reliable solutions in modern dentistry. They are designed to replace missing teeth with strong, durable, and natural-looking alternatives that function just like real teeth. A permanent medical-grade titanium or zirconia post anchored directly in the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. Topped with a lifelike custom ceramic crown, it restores full biting power and never slips.
