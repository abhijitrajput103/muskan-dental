What Is a Dental Implant Bridge?

A Dental Implant Bridge is an advanced restorative option designed to replace three or more missing teeth in a row. Unlike a traditional dental bridge—which requires shaving down adjacent healthy teeth—an implant bridge uses titanium implant posts anchored directly into the jawbone to secure the bridge. This structure restores full chewing capability, prevents bone loss, preserves natural facial symmetry, and provides a seamless, natural aesthetic without affecting neighboring healthy teeth.

Treatment Journey

Implant Bridge Process

1. Evaluation & Digital Planning

Comprehensive oral assessment, 3D CBCT scanning, and digital placement planning.

2. Implant Placement

Surgical placement of titanium implant posts into the jawbone as anchor points.

3. Healing & Osseointegration

Healing period allowing the implants to fuse with surrounding bone tissue.

4. Final Bridge Attachment

Custom-made bridge secured onto the implants for a permanent restored smile.
