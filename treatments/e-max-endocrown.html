What Is an E-Max Endocrown?

E-max Endocrowns provide a modern, minimally invasive alternative to traditional post-and-core supported full crowns for post-endodontic restorations. Fabricated from a single block of high-strength lithium disilicate glass-ceramic, an endocrown utilizes the internal geometry of the cleaned pulp chamber for retention. By retaining remaining coronal tooth structure and eliminating the mechanical stress associated with metal or fiber posts, E-max endocrowns offer superior resistance to fracture, excellent marginal integrity, and exceptional long-term structural integrity for heavily restored posterior teeth.
